ChillOut gives you

A platform where anyone can create an event

Create and manage events exactly how you want with complete flexibility over visibility and access. Whether it's a public event for open participation, a private event for exclusive access, or specialized events like women-only or paid events, ChillOut gives you full control. Set custom restrictions, secure entry with QR codes for event entry.. No matter the event type, you can create events as your preferences.

Event Chat Box

Each event has its own chat room where you can easily connect with others. Have questions about the event? Need details or updates? Just jump in the chat! You can also meet new people, share tips, or make plans before and after the event. It's the perfect place to stay on line and build connections.

Manage Attendees: View, Approve, or Reject Requests for Your Event

Easily manage attendees for your event with full control. For private events, view requests, approve trusted participants , or reject others , you can also view other profiles and make decisions. For public events, anyone can join instantly, making it open and accessible to all.

Know the People by Their Profile and Verification Status

View user profiles and check verification status to understand who you are connecting with. Verified profiles display a special badge, and company name attached to profile, making them more trustworthy. Verifying with your company email promotes safer, genuine interactions.
